This folder contains the ontology and manuscript sources for the Æther-Flow research program.

The scientific goal of the project is to derive General Relativity (GR) from the Æther-Flow ontology. The research is carried out with **Sys4AI**, an agentic research system that coordinates AI agents, roles, skills, research tasks, and verification processes. Sys4AI provides the research infrastructure; the ontology provides the scientific starting point.

The comprehensive TeX source and its compiled PDF are the **canonical versions** of the ontology manuscript. The individual manuscripts are available in `aether_flow_manuscript-sets/`.

## What is the Æther-Flow Ontology?

The Æther-Flow ontology explores the physical reality behind the spacetime described by Einstein's General Relativity. It proposes that the space and passage of time we experience arise from ordered motion within an underlying four-dimensional structure called the Æther. The goal of the project is to derive General Relativity (GR) from the Æther-Flow ontology.
